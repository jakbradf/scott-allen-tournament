Format & Scoring

Fourball — modified Stableford

Players compete in teams of two and as individuals at the same time. It is a fourball: on every hole, the better of the two team members’ scores counts toward the team total.

Scoring is modified Stableford, with extra reward for birdies, eagles and better. Points are awarded on your gross score on each hole:

Bogey1
Par2
Birdie4
Eagle6
Albatross8
Hole‑in‑One10

Double bogey or worse scores 0 points on the hole — pick up and move on.

Two scoring rounds

Category 1 — Gross

Most Stableford points collected over the eighteen holes. No handicap adjustment — raw points, best player wins.

Category 2 — Net

Your Stableford points plus your handicap, added at the end. This levels the field between low and high handicappers.

For example: a player off 18 who records eighteen bogeys scores 18 points + 18 = 36. A scratch player who records eighteen pars scores 36 + 0 = 36. Even race.

The Course

Drøbak Golfklubb

Founded in 1988 with the full eighteen opening in 1992, Drøbak Golfklubb is an award‑winning par 70 parkland course set into wooded, rolling ground near the Oslofjord. It measures a compact 5,138 metres — short on the card, but tight, tree‑lined and well‑bunkered, with water in play early on the 2nd. The back nine is unusual: not a single par 5, so points are there to be won on the front.
